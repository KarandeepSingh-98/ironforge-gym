# Feature 03 — About Section

## Objective
Introduce IRONFORGE FITNESS and communicate its philosophy, experience and community.

## Content
Explain:
- Who the gym is.
- Training philosophy.
- Quality of equipment.
- Trainer expertise.
- Community atmosphere.

## Statistics
Use realistic placeholder statistics such as:
- 10+ Years Experience
- 2,500+ Members
- 25+ Expert Trainers
- 50+ Weekly Classes

## Requirements
- Image/visual.
- Heading and supporting content.
- Statistics.
- Optional CTA.
- Responsive two-column layout that becomes one column on mobile.

## Design
Maintain the overall premium fitness aesthetic.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Content is easy to scan.
- Statistics are visually distinct.
- Image does not break responsive layout.
- Section has consistent spacing.
- Accessibility basics are satisfied.
